# Free download collection

The 19 templates are provided for free download by Arclayer, as requested by Rezan Ferdous. The catalog price is zero. This package includes the actual source and bundled files, rather than screenshots alone.

Existing third-party font and asset notices remain applicable. Reference clips and images identified in prompts are included only where recovered, with their provenance. No new blanket third-party license or exclusive ownership claim is created by the handoff.
